# Next Gig - Monetization Strategy & Premium Features

**Document Created**: November 29, 2025
**Current Status**: Free-to-use platform
**Goal**: Convert to freemium model with tiered subscriptions

---

## Executive Summary

Next Gig currently aggregates jobs from LinkedIn, IfYouCould, UN Jobs, and Workable, providing users with a centralized job search experience. This document outlines a comprehensive monetization strategy that adds value for paying users while maintaining a functional free tier.

## Proposed Tiered Subscription Model

### 🆓 **FREE Tier** (Current Experience with Limits)
**Price**: £0/month
**Target**: Job seekers who want basic access

#### Features:
- ✅ Access to 2 job sources (choose from LinkedIn or IfYouCould)
- ✅ View jobs from last 24 hours only
- ✅ Basic search (title and company only)
- ✅ Manual job tracking (mark as applied)
- ✅ 1 saved job title preference
- ✅ 1 saved location preference
- 🚫 View up to 20 jobs per day (daily limit)
- 🚫 No email notifications
- 🚫 No advanced filters

**Conversion Triggers**:
- "Upgrade to see 50+ more jobs matching your criteria"
- "Premium users get notified within 1 hour of new job postings"
- "Unlock advanced filters to find your perfect role faster"

---

### 💎 **PREMIUM Tier** (Core Paid Offering)
**Price**: £4.99/month or £49/year (save £10)
**Target**: Active job seekers who want competitive advantage

#### Everything in FREE, plus:

#### Job Discovery Enhanced
- ✅ **All 4 job sources** (LinkedIn, IfYouCould, UN Jobs, Workable) + future sources
- ✅ **Unlimited job viewing** (no daily limits)
- ✅ **View all jobs** (not just last 24 hours)
- ✅ **Advanced search** (search by description, skills, keywords)
- ✅ **Priority access** (see new jobs 30 minutes before free users)

#### Smart Filters & Alerts
- ✅ **Advanced filters**:
  - Salary range
  - Remote/Hybrid/Office
  - Company size
  - Job type (full-time, part-time, contract)
  - Experience level
  - Benefits offered
  - Date posted range
- ✅ **Email notifications** (daily digest of new matching jobs)
- ✅ **Saved searches** (up to 5)
- ✅ **Custom job alerts** (get notified when specific criteria match)

#### Organization & Tracking
- ✅ **Unlimited job title preferences** (vs 1 in free)
- ✅ **Unlimited location preferences** (vs 1 in free)
- ✅ **Job bookmarks/saves** (unlimited)
- ✅ **Application tracking dashboard**:
  - Track application status (applied, interviewing, rejected, offered)
  - Add notes to jobs
  - Set follow-up reminders
  - View application timeline
- ✅ **Export job lists** (CSV, PDF)

#### Insights
- ✅ **Job market insights**:
  - Trending job titles in your area
  - Average salary ranges
  - Most active companies hiring
  - Best days/times to apply
- ✅ **Personal analytics**:
  - Application success rate
  - Time to hear back
  - Most successful application times

## Revenue Projections

### Conservative Estimate (Year 1)

**Assumptions**:
- 10,000 total users
- 5% conversion to Premium (500 users)
- 1% conversion to Professional (100 users)
- 80% monthly billing, 20% annual billing

**Monthly Revenue**:
- Premium: (400 × £4.99) + (100 × £4.08) = £1,996 + £408 = **£2,404/month**
- Professional: (80 × £9.99) + (20 × £8.25) = £799 + £165 = **£964/month**
- **Total: £3,368/month** or **£40,416/year**

### Moderate Estimate (Year 2)

**Assumptions**:
- 50,000 total users
- 8% conversion to Premium (4,000 users)
- 2% conversion to Professional (1,000 users)

**Monthly Revenue**:
- Premium: (3,200 × £4.99) + (800 × £4.08) = £15,968 + £3,264 = **£19,232/month**
- Professional: (800 × £9.99) + (200 × £8.25) = £7,992 + £1,650 = **£9,642/month**
- **Total: £28,874/month** or **£346,488/year**

### Optimistic Estimate (Year 3)

**Assumptions**:
- 200,000 total users
- 10% conversion to Premium (20,000 users)
- 3% conversion to Professional (6,000 users)

**Monthly Revenue**:
- Premium: (16,000 × £4.99) + (4,000 × £4.08) = £79,840 + £16,320 = **£96,160/month**
- Professional: (4,800 × £9.99) + (1,200 × £8.25) = £47,952 + £9,900 = **£57,852/month**
- **Total: £154,012/month** or **£1,848,144/year**

## Conversion Optimization Strategies

### 1. Limited-Time Offers
- First 1,000 users: 50% off for life
- Launch special: 3 months for the price of 2
- Black Friday / Cyber Monday deals
- Student discounts (20% off with .ac.uk email)

### 2. Freemium Friction Points
- Show "20/20 jobs viewed today" counter
- "Upgrade to unlock 47 more jobs matching your criteria"

- Free users see stats: "Premium users find jobs 3x faster"
- Showcase success stories from premium members
- Free 7-day trial of Premium tier
- Show job count difference: "200 jobs available vs 20 in free tier"

### 4. Social Proof
- Display active premium member count
- Show testimonials from successful job seekers
- "Join 5,000+ professionals finding their dream jobs"

### 5. Urgency & FOMO
- "New job posted 5 minutes ago (Premium only)"
- "This job received 50+ applications in the last hour"
- "Premium users were notified 30 minutes before you"

## Alternative Monetization Models

### Option 1: Pay-Per-Application Model
Instead of subscription, charge per job application assistance:
- £0.99 per AI-generated cover letter
- £1.99 per application autofill
- £4.99 per interview prep package
- **Pros**: Lower barrier to entry, impulse purchases
- **Cons**: Less predictable revenue, may feel transactional

### Option 2: Freemium + Credits
Hybrid model:
- Free users get 10 credits/month
- Premium = Unlimited credits
- Actions cost credits:
  - View job details: 1 credit
  - Save job: 1 credit
  - Get AI recommendation: 2 credits
  - Export job list: 5 credits
- Buy credit packs: £4.99 for 50 credits
- **Pros**: Flexibility, multiple revenue streams
- **Cons**: Complicated UX, harder to predict value

### Option 3: Enterprise/Recruiter Tier
B2B offering:
- £99-£499/month for recruiters
- Post jobs directly to your platform
- Access to candidate pool
- Analytics on job performance
- **Pros**: Higher revenue per customer, new market
- **Cons**: Different target audience, requires sales team

## Risk Mitigation

### Potential Risks
1. **Free users leave**: Make free tier valuable but limited
2. **Low conversion**: A/B test pricing, offer trials, improve value props
3. **High churn**: Focus on job placement success, add sticky features
4. **Technical debt**: Phase implementation carefully
5. **Job source changes**: Diversify sources, have backup scrapers

### Contingency Plans
- If conversion <3%: Extend free trials, adjust pricing
- If churn >7%: Survey churned users, improve features
- If costs exceed revenue: Reduce free tier further, increase prices
- If job sources fail: Have 6+ sources minimum for redundancy
